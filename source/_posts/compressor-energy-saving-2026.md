# Factory Compressor Energy Saving Guide: From Leak Detection to Heat Recovery

Air compressors are among the most energy-consuming equipment in factories. A 200kW compressor can cost over 1.5 million RMB annually in electricity. This article shares practical compressor energy-saving experience.

## 1. How High Is Compressor Electricity Cost?

**Calculation:** Power (kW) × Operating Hours × Electricity Rate

- 200kW × 8,000 hours × 0.8 RMB/kWh = **1.28 million RMB/year**

This is under ideal conditions; actual costs are often higher.

## 2. Three Major Energy Saving Pitfalls

### 1. Leakage: The Invisible Electricity Monster

**Impact:** 1mm diameter hole wastes 15,000 kWh per year

**Detection Methods:**
- Ultrasonic leak detector
- Listen for "hissing" sounds at night
- Check loading time ratio

**Standard:** Handle if leakage exceeds 10%

### 2. Improper Sizing

**Principle:** Maintain 75%-90% load rate

**Recommendations:**
- Below 50% for long term, consider smaller units or VFD models
- One-primary-one-standby saves more than two running simultaneously

### 3. Wasted Heat

70% of compressor electrical energy converts to heat.

**Heat Recovery Uses:**
- Dormitory heating
- Process water preheating
- Workshop auxiliary heating

Payback period: 1-2 years.

## 3. Energy Saving Indicators

| Indicator | Normal Value | Warning Signal |
|-----------|--------------|----------------|
| Loading Rate | 85%-95% | Below 50% |
| Discharge Temp | 75-85°C | Too high |
| Oil Consumption | <3ppm | Too high |
| Noise | <75dB | Too high |

## 4. Implementation Recommendations

1. **New Factory Planning:** Hire professionals for sizing calculation
2. **Retrofit Projects:** Prioritize VFD and heat recovery
3. **Daily Management:** Regular leak detection
4. **Spare Parts:** Based on historical maintenance records
