# Factory Power System: The Hidden Problems 80% of Facility Engineers Have Encountered

In IC packaging and testing factories, the power system is critical infrastructure. However, small issues can become major problems. This article shares common hidden dangers and solutions in power operations.

## 1. Busbar Screw Loosening: A Real Lesson

Our factory once experienced a complete power failure at 3 AM. After 40 minutes of troubleshooting, we found the cause was a loose busbar screw in one cabinet.

**Problem Chain:** Heat → Screw loosens → Contact resistance increases → More heat → Insulation breakdown

**Solutions:**
- Add busbar tightening to quarterly inspection checklist
- Use thermal imaging camera for key connection temperature checks

## 2. Transformer Three-Phase Imbalance: The Overlooked "Chronic Disease"

Transformer three-phase imbalance exceeding 15% is common but often overlooked.

**Hazards:**
1. Transformer capacity reduced to 70%
2. Increased line losses
3. Motor overheating, shortened lifespan
4. Aggravated harmonic problems

**Solutions:**
- Regularly measure three-phase current, calculate imbalance rate
- Adjust load distribution, control within 10%
- Consider installing three-phase balancing devices

After balancing, our transformer temperature dropped 8°C on average, with line losses decreasing by 3%.

## 3. Spare Parts Management: Precision is Key

Spare parts management is challenging for many factories. Too many parts tie up capital; too few causes problems.

**On-Demand Strategy:**
- Analyze 5 years of failure/replacement records
- List frequently replaced part numbers
- Stock high-frequency items
- Use vendor consignment for others

This reduced inventory by 60% while improving response speed.

## 4. Digital Transformation: Data is Foundation

**Energy Management System:**
- 80+ meters with automatic data collection
- Real-time monitoring and analysis
- Abnormal power consumption alerts

**Predictive Maintenance:**
- Online transformer oil temperature monitoring
- Cabinet temperature monitoring
- Key circuit power factor monitoring

## 5. HVAC Systems: The Energy Consumption Giant

HVAC accounts for 30-50% of total factory energy consumption, even over 60% in cleanrooms.

**Key Energy Saving Points:**
1. VFD transformation: Save 20-30%
2. Optimize control logic: Temperature-humidity linkage
3. Waste heat recovery: 2-3 year payback
4. Regular cleaning: Improve COP by 15-20%
