# Motor Predictive Maintenance: Let Equipment Failures Speak for Themselves

## Why Predictive Maintenance Matters

A food factory's cold storage compressor motor suddenly burned out, causing 3 days of production halt and direct losses exceeding 500,000 RMB. Post-analysis revealed the motor had shown obvious warning signs half a year before the failure: increased vibration, unstable current, and abnormal noise.

If interventions had been made at that time, this accident could have been completely avoided.

This is the value of predictive maintenance—not waiting for equipment to fail before repairing, but letting equipment "speak for itself" about problems.

## 5 Warning Signs of Motor Failures

### 1. Abnormal Vibration

**Normal Range:** ≤4.5mm/s (RMS value)
**Warning Threshold:** ≥7.1mm/s

**Detection Methods:**
- Regular testing with handheld vibration meters
- Real-time monitoring with online vibration sensors

**Fault Correspondence:**
- Bearing wear → High-frequency vibration
- Imbalance → Fundamental frequency vibration
- Misalignment → 2x frequency vibration

### 2. Temperature Increase

**Normal Range:** Temperature rise not exceeding 80% of rated temperature
**Warning Threshold:** Temperature rise exceeding 90% of rated temperature

**Detection Methods:**
- Regular scanning with thermal imaging cameras
- Continuous monitoring with online temperature sensors

**Fault Correspondence:**
- Bearing issues → Increased bearing housing temperature
- Winding issues → Increased motor body temperature
- Poor heat dissipation → Overall elevated temperature

### 3. Current Fluctuation

**Normal Range:** Current stays within ±5% of rated value
**Warning Threshold:** Current fluctuation exceeding ±10%

**Detection Methods:**
- Electrical parameter analyzers
- Online power quality monitoring

**Fault Correspondence:**
- Rotor issues → Current waveform distortion
- Partial winding short → Unstable current
- Abnormal load → Current too high or too low

### 4. Noise Changes

**Normal State:** Running sound is uniform and stable
**Warning Signal:** Abnormal sounds, whistling, metal friction sounds

**Detection Methods:**
- Regular inspections by listening
- Ultrasonic detectors (for high frequencies beyond human hearing)

**Fault Correspondence:**
- Bearing damage → Rattling, clicking sounds
- Fan issues → Whistling sounds
- Loose parts → Impact sounds

### 5. Insulation Deterioration

**Normal Value:** Insulation resistance ≥ 100MΩ
**Warning Value:** Insulation resistance < 10MΩ

**Detection Methods:**
- Insulation resistance testers (Megohmmeter)
- Regular preventive testing

**Fault Correspondence:**
- Moisture → Sharp drop in insulation resistance
- Aging → Gradual decline in insulation resistance
- Contamination → Unstable insulation resistance

## Implementation Path for Predictive Maintenance

### Phase 1: Data Collection (1-2 months)

**Key Parameters:**
- Vibration (displacement/velocity/acceleration)
- Temperature (bearing temperature, winding temperature, ambient temperature)
- Current (RMS, peak, waveform)
- Noise (sound pressure level, frequency spectrum)

**Collection Frequency:**
- Critical equipment: Real-time online monitoring
- Important equipment: Weekly patrol testing
- General equipment: Monthly regular testing

### Phase 2: Trend Analysis (2-3 months)

**Analysis Methods:**
1. Establish equipment health baseline
2. Track parameter change trends
3. Compare with similar equipment data
4. Combine with historical maintenance records

**Warning Models:**
- Single parameter threshold alarms
- Multi-parameter comprehensive assessment
- AI machine learning prediction

### Phase 3: Intelligent Warning (Continuous Optimization)

| Level | Status | Recommended Response Time |
|-------|--------|--------------------------|
| Green | Normal | Regular maintenance |
| Blue | Slight abnormality | Inspect within 1 week |
| Yellow | Moderate abnormality | Handle within 3 days |
| Red | Severe abnormality | Stop immediately for inspection |

## Implementation Recommendations

### Selection Guide

**Vibration Sensors:**
- Accelerometers: High-frequency vibration monitoring
- Displacement sensors: Low-speed equipment monitoring
- Recommended brands: SKF, EMERSON, B&R

**Temperature Sensors:**
- PT100: High precision, strong versatility
- Thermocouples: Fast response speed
- Infrared thermal cameras: Quick screening

**Analysis Software:**
- Professional CMMS systems
- Cloud-based data analysis platforms
- Visual dashboard displays

### Promotion Strategy

1. **Pilot First:** Select 1-2 critical equipment for pilot testing
2. **Gradual Rollout:** Expand to more equipment after success
3. **Continuous Optimization:** Adjust warning thresholds based on actual effects
