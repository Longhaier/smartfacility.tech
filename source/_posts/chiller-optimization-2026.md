# IC Factory Chiller System Optimization: Practical Experience Saving 50% Summer Electricity Bills

Summer is here, and chillers (water chillers) become the main electricity expense. A 1000kW chiller running for one day costs about 16,000 RMB. This article shares practical chiller energy optimization experience.

## 1. How High Is Chiller Electricity Cost?

**Daily Operating Cost:**
- 1,000kW × 20 hours × 0.8 RMB/kWh = **16,000 RMB/day**
- Approximately **480,000 RMB per month**

## 2. Common Problem Analysis

### 1. Oversizing "Large Horse Pulling Small Car"

- Selected for maximum load, usually unnecessary
- Large production fluctuations, summer/winter load differs by 50%
- Long-term low-load operation, low efficiency

### 2. Inefficient Cooling Tower

**Common Problems:**
- Uneven water distribution
- Aging filler
- Loose fan belts
- Pipe scaling

**Impact:** 10% efficiency loss = 5% more electricity cost

### 3. Rough Control Strategy

- Temperature set and forgotten
- Cooling pumps/towers always running at full capacity
- Lack of group control optimization

## 3. Energy Saving Solutions

### 1. Chiller VFD Transformation

- Add VFD to centrifugal chillers
- Dynamically adjust power based on load
- **Save over 15% on average**

### 2. Cooling Tower Fan VFD

- Adjust fan speed based on cooling water temperature
- **Save 5%-8%**

### 3. Group Control Optimization

- Link chiller, cooling tower, and cooling pump
- Dynamically adjust based on outdoor wet bulb temperature
- **Save hundreds of thousands annually**

### 4. Regular Heat Exchanger Cleaning

- COP improves 10%-15% after cleaning
- Clean at least once per year

## 4. Maintenance Warning Signs

| Phenomenon | Possible Problem |
|------------|------------------|
| Small inlet/outlet temp difference | Inefficient cooling tower/scaling |
| High operating current | Refrigerant abnormality |
| Sudden loud noise | Bearing wear |
| Abnormal vibration | Loose foundation |

## 5. Summary

Key chiller energy saving points:
1. Don't oversize during selection
2. VFD transformation is highest priority
3. Implement group control
4. Regular maintenance is essential
