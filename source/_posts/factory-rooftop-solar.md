# Factory Rooftop Solar PV: Is It Worth Installing? Do the Math First

## Solar Prices Have Dropped—Should You Jump In?

Solar panel prices have been continuously declining recently, and many factory owners are eager to try. But I want to remind everyone: you can install it, but you must calculate the numbers clearly.

Last year, a friend installed solar PV at their factory, only to discover that annual power generation was 30% lower than expected, extending the investment payback period from 5 to 8 years.

Today, let's discuss how to calculate the economics of factory rooftop solar.

## How to Calculate Investment Costs

### System Cost Breakdown

| Component | Proportion | Unit Price Reference |
|-----------|------------|---------------------|
| PV modules | 45-50% | 1.2-1.5 RMB/W |
| Inverters | 10-12% | 0.3-0.5 RMB/W |
| Mounting system | 8-10% | 0.2-0.3 RMB/W |
| Cables and distribution | 5-8% | 0.15-0.2 RMB/W |
| Installation labor | 10-15% | 0.3-0.5 RMB/W |
| Others | 10-15% | - |

**2024 Mainstream Price:** 3.0-3.5 RMB/W (turnkey price)

### Capacity Calculation Formula

**Formula:**
> Installed capacity (kWp) = Usable roof area (m²) × 100-120W/m² × efficiency factor

**Example:** 10,000m² roof, effective area 8,000m²
> 8,000 × 110 × 0.85 = 748kWp (approximately 750kWp)

## How to Calculate Returns

### Power Generation Estimation

**Annual Power Generation Formula:**
> Annual generation = Installed capacity × annual equivalent full-load hours

**Annual Equivalent Full-Load Hours by Region:**

| Region | Hours |
|--------|-------|
| Category I resource area (Tibet, Qinghai) | 1600-2000h |
| Category II resource area (Gansu, Ningxia) | 1300-1600h |
| Category III resource area (Shandong, Henan) | 1000-1300h |
| Category IV resource area (Sichuan, Hubei) | 800-1000h |

**Example:** 750kWp installation in Category III region
> 750 × 1100 = 825,000 kWh/year

### Revenue Composition

| Revenue Source | Calculation Method |
|---------------|-------------------|
| Electricity savings | Generation × electricity price |
| Feed-in revenue | Excess electricity × feed-in price |
| Subsidy benefits | Local policy |

**Using Category III region as example:**
- Annual generation: 825,000 kWh
- Self-consumption ratio: 70% (577,500 kWh)
- Grid feed-in ratio: 30% (247,500 kWh)
- Electricity price: 0.6 RMB/kWh
- Feed-in price: 0.35 RMB/kWh
- **Annual revenue: 577,500 × 0.6 + 247,500 × 0.35 = 447,000 RMB**

## Investment Return Analysis

### Static Payback Period

**Formula:**
> Static payback period = Total investment ÷ Annual revenue

**Example:**
- Total investment: 750kWp × 3.2 RMB/W = 2.4 million RMB
- Annual revenue: 447,000 RMB
- **Static payback period: 2,400,000 ÷ 447,000 = 5.4 years**

### Considering Degradation

**PV module degradation规律:**
- Year 1: 2-3%
- Subsequent years: 0.5-0.7% annually

**Revenue after 10 years:**
> First year revenue × (1 - average annual degradation)^10

**Calculating for 10 years:**
- Total revenue over 10 years: approximately 3.8 million RMB
- After deducting investment: 3.8 - 2.4 = 1.4 million RMB
- **Actual IRR: approximately 8-10%**

## Which Factories Are Suitable

### Suitable Factories

1. **Large roof area** - Above 5,000m²
2. **Clear property rights** - Self-owned factory, no disputes
3. **High electricity consumption** - Annual electricity bills above 1 million RMB
4. **Mainly daytime usage** - Solar generation matches electricity usage periods
5. **Local subsidies available** - Regional subsidy policies

### Not Suitable Factories

1. **Old roof structure** - Requires significant reinforcement
2. **Mainly nighttime electricity usage** - Solar generation cannot be used
3. **Long-term shading on roof** - Trees, building shading
4. **Unstable lease terms** - Landlord may change
5. **Very cheap electricity prices** - Investment return takes too long

## Pitfall Prevention Guide

### Common Schemes

1. **Exaggerating power generation** - Claiming 1500h/year actual only 1000h
2. **Low-bidding with inferior materials** - Using substandard components
3. **Hidden O&M costs** - Free O&M for first few years, then high fees
4. **Lack of installation qualifications** - Non-professional team construction

### Selection Recommendations

**Module Brands:**
- Tier 1: LONGi, Trina Solar, Jinko Solar
- Tier 2: Canadian Solar, Risen Energy
- Avoid: Unknown brands, low-priced Grade B panels

**Inverter Brands:**
- Huawei, Sungrow, GoodWe
- Focus on: Conversion efficiency, after-sales service

**Installers:**
- Check past projects
- Verify qualification certificates
- Clarify warranty period

## Implementation Process

### 6 Steps

1. **Roof assessment** - Structural load capacity, shading conditions
2. **Demand calculation** - Electricity consumption, installation capacity
3. **Proposal comparison** - Compare 3-5 quotations
4. **Contract signing** - Clarify rights and responsibilities
5. **Construction and installation** - Supervise construction quality
6. **Grid connection acceptance** - Handle grid connection procedures

### Grid Connection Application

**Required Materials:**
- Property certificate
- Business license
- Roof structure diagram
- Electrical single-line diagram
- Investor information

**Processing Department:** Local power grid company business hall

## Summary

Factory rooftop solar is worth installing, but you must calculate the numbers clearly.

**Key Points:**
- Investment payback period 5-7 years, IRR approximately 8-10%
- Suitable for factories with high electricity consumption and daytime usage
- Choose tier-1 brands and professional installers
- Calculate power generation clearly before deciding

Solar is a good project, but don't rush in without thinking it through.
